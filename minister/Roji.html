Roji M. John

Roji M. John is an Indian politician and a prominent leader of the Indian National Congress from Kerala. He represents the Angamaly constituency in the Kerala Legislative Assembly and is known for his active involvement in public service and developmental initiatives. Born on 28 October 1978, he entered politics through student organizations and gradually established himself as a dynamic leader committed to the welfare of the people.

Throughout his political career, Roji M. John has focused on issues related to education, infrastructure development, youth welfare, employment generation, and social justice. He has actively participated in legislative discussions and has worked towards improving public services and development projects in his constituency. His leadership and dedication have earned him recognition among people across Kerala.

Following the 2026 Kerala Legislative Assembly Elections, Roji M. John was elected as a Member of the Kerala Legislative Assembly and became a member of the Council of Ministers in the Government of Kerala. As a Cabinet Minister, he is involved in implementing policies aimed at improving governance, public welfare, and sustainable development across the state.

Known for his approachable nature, administrative abilities, and commitment to public service, Roji M. John continues to play an important role in Kerala's political landscape. His efforts are focused on promoting inclusive growth, strengthening public infrastructure, and enhancing opportunities for the younger generation.
