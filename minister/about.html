About Kerala Government

The Government of Kerala is dedicated to providing transparent, efficient, and citizen-friendly governance. Through its various ministries and departments, the government works to promote education, healthcare, infrastructure development, social welfare, agriculture, technology, and environmental sustainability. The Kerala Ministers Portal serves as an information platform that helps citizens learn about the Council of Ministers, their responsibilities, government initiatives, and public welfare programs. The government remains committed to inclusive growth, sustainable development, and improving the quality of life for all people of Kerala through effective policies, innovation, and public service.
