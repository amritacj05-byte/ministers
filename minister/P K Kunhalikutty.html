P. K. Kunhalikutty

P. K. Kunhalikutty is one of the most experienced and influential political leaders in Kerala. He is a senior leader of the Indian Union Muslim League (IUML) and has played a major role in the political development of the state for more than four decades. Born on 1 June 1951 in Malappuram district, he entered politics through student organizations and gradually rose to become one of the most respected leaders of the IUML. His leadership skills, administrative experience, and commitment to public service have made him a prominent figure in Kerala politics.

Throughout his political career, Kunhalikutty has served as a Member of the Kerala Legislative Assembly multiple times and has represented constituencies such as Kuttippuram, Vengara, and Malappuram. He has also served as a Member of Parliament in the Lok Sabha representing the Malappuram constituency. His long political journey reflects his strong connection with the people and his ability to address their concerns effectively.

During various United Democratic Front (UDF) governments, P. K. Kunhalikutty held important ministerial portfolios including Industries, Information Technology, Social Welfare, Municipal Administration, Haj and Wakf Affairs. He played a significant role in promoting industrial growth, technological development, digital governance, and welfare programs across Kerala. His contributions to the IT sector and industrial development have been widely recognized.

Following the 2026 Kerala Legislative Assembly elections, P. K. Kunhalikutty emerged as one of the senior-most leaders in the newly formed UDF government. He won from the Malappuram constituency and became a member of the Kerala Cabinet. In 2026, he serves as the Minister for Industries, Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence in the Government of Kerala. He is also a senior leader and National General Secretary of the Indian Union Muslim League.
