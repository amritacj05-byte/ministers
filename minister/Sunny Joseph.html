Sunny Joseph

Adv. Sunny Joseph is a senior Indian politician, advocate, and public leader from Kerala. He is a prominent member of the Indian National Congress and has represented the Peravoor constituency in the Kerala Legislative Assembly for multiple terms. Born on 18 August 1952 in Thodupuzha, Kerala, he completed his Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Law degrees before entering public life. Sunny Joseph began his political career through student and youth organizations associated with the Congress party. Over the years, he held several important positions within the party organization and became one of the most respected Congress leaders in Kerala. He served as District Congress Committee President in Kannur and played a major role in strengthening the United Democratic Front (UDF) in the region. As a legislator, Sunny Joseph has actively worked for the development of Peravoor and surrounding regions. His efforts have focused on improving infrastructure, education, healthcare, rural development, agriculture, and public welfare. He has consistently raised public issues in the Kerala Legislative Assembly and advocated for policies that improve the quality of life of citizens. In 2025, Sunny Joseph was appointed President of the Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC), becoming the head of the Congress party in Kerala. Under his leadership, the party strengthened its organizational structure and prepared for the 2026 Kerala Assembly Elections. Following the 2026 Kerala Assembly Elections, Sunny Joseph was re-elected from the Peravoor constituency and became an important member of the newly formed UDF government. Reports indicated that he was likely to join the Kerala Cabinet after the election victory.
