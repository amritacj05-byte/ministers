K. Muraleedharan

K. Muraleedharan (Kannoth Muraleedharan) is a senior Indian politician, lawyer, and social worker from Kerala. Born on 14 May 1957 in Thrissur, he is the son of former Kerala Chief Minister K. Karunakaran, one of the most influential leaders in Kerala's political history. Muraleedharan has been an active participant in public life for several decades and is widely recognized for his contributions to politics, governance, and social development. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

He began his political career through the Indian National Congress and gradually emerged as one of the party's prominent leaders in Kerala. Throughout his career, he has served as a Member of Parliament, Member of the Kerala Legislative Assembly, and President of the Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC). His experience in both state and national politics has made him a respected leader known for his administrative skills and public engagement. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

K. Muraleedharan has represented several constituencies in both the Lok Sabha and the Kerala Legislative Assembly. He served as Member of Parliament from Kozhikode and Vadakara constituencies and later represented Vattiyoorkavu in the Kerala Legislative Assembly. He also served as Minister for Electricity in the Government of Kerala, where he focused on improving power infrastructure and public services. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Following the 2026 Kerala Assembly Elections, K. Muraleedharan won from the Vattiyoorkavu constituency as an Indian National Congress candidate and became a member of the Kerala Legislative Assembly. Known for his dedication to public service, commitment to democratic values, and strong leadership qualities, K. Muraleedharan remains an influential figure in Kerala politics. His focus on education, infrastructure development, social welfare, and transparent governance has earned him recognition and respect among people across the state.
